Method & limitations

Visualisation of logged missions, not real footage. The descent is illustrative: missions start at the home station. Terrain is smoothed between grid cells and its height is exaggerated 3×. Sediment texture, lighting, marine snow and vehicle models are decorative, and vehicles aren't to scale. Camera feed cards show simulated encounters. Light columns mark a robot's first survey of a habitat class. Habitat classes are clustered from terrain for display only: they aren't biomes, and planners never see them.
